# DIRECTION: Encore project pack

## 1. КОНЦЕПТ
A CTO, lead engineer or recruiter opens one file on a laptop, with no setup, and within a minute knows what this is, what it proves, and where it stops. Then each role digs as deep as it wants.

## 2. МОТИВ
The wire: a thin line with nodes, carried over from the Schematic console. It lives in the navigation rail, the diagrams and the dot field of the hero.

## 3. НАСТРІЙ
Exact, not cold. Dense, not cramped. Honest, not apologetic. It is not a marketing landing page and not a slide deck.

## 4. ПРИНЦИПИ
White and graphite with one blue accent (#1a36f5), no beige. Geist and Geist Mono, embedded. Numbers in mono with tabular figures. All numbers come from result files at build time. Tokens shared with the console, in both themes.

## 5. РЕФЕРЕНСИ
The Encore console itself, direction C Schematic, chosen by the owner.

## 6. ЧОГО НЕМАЄ
No hype words, no stock images, no gradients as decoration, no claims without a file behind them.
